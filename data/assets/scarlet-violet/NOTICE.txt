Pokemon menu icons from https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites
Upstream revision: c0542f7c51215d9672439012838c99f520596cbd

This package covers all 1,350 Pokemon records, including forms, in the pinned
application catalog. It selects exactly one unmodified menu PNG for each ID.
Scarlet/Violet is preferred (869 records). Missing IDs are covered by matching
IDs from Champions (140), Legends Z-A (88), Legends Arceus (39), BDSP (60),
Sword/Shield (132) and Generation VII (22). No species substitution is used.

The five held-item icons use upstream standard item images required by the
current item selector. The nineteen type badges use Scarlet/Violet images.
The exact original paths, deployed paths, dimensions and SHA-256 checksums
are recorded in manifest.json. Animation, shiny variants and large artwork
are excluded. PNG payloads total 20,079,618 bytes across 1,374 assets.
